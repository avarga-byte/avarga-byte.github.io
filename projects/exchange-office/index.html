Exchange Office Manager

An operations dashboard for a currency exchange business — rates, accountancy, and expenses in one place.

↑ A working React + Tailwind replica of the operations dashboard with seed data — move through the overview, live rates & transactions, expenses, and admins in the sidebar. The original build's login screen ↗

Overview

Built for a currency exchange office, this dashboard replaces spreadsheets with a single system for the day-to-day of running an exchange counter. It has production access logs from real use.

What it does

  • Staff log in and manage live exchange rates against a full currency and bank list.
  • An accountancy module tracks the office's books.
  • An expense module records day-to-day operating costs.
  • A dashboard view surfaces the numbers that matter for daily operations at a glance.

Why it's interesting

It's a domain-specific back-office tool — currency exchange has its own accounting conventions, and the routes are organized around admin, accountancy, exchange, and expense rather than a generic CRUD structure.
