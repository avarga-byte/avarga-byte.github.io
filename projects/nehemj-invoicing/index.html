Web App

Nehemj Invoicing

A contract, invoice, and payment management tool with official stamp generation and PDF export.

↑ A working React + Tailwind replica — edit the line items on the left and watch the stamped invoice update live on the right. The original build's login screen ↗

Overview

"Nehemjlel" is the Mongolian word for invoice — this tool was built to replace manual paperwork for a services business, generating contracts and invoices complete with an official stamp/seal image, ready to hand to a client.

What it does

  • Create and manage contracts tied to a client and a payment schedule.
  • Generate invoices with currency-aware formatting.
  • Export finished documents to PDF, stamped with the company seal, for printing or emailing directly to clients.

Why it's interesting

It pairs naturally with the SkyKing Security project — a guard-services company needs exactly this kind of contract and invoicing tool for its customer accounts.

Tech Stack
