Overview

Built for a product promotion campaign, this app turns purchase verification into a game: customers scan a code from a product, collect entries, and find out if they've won.

What it does

  • A built-in scanner reads promotional codes and registers an entry for the account.
  • Entries accumulate as raffle tickets, shown in a prize list screen.
  • Dedicated "You Won" / "You Lose" result screens for drawing outcomes.
  • Push notifications (via Expo's push service) alert users to results and new promotions.
  • An "Invite a Friend" flow for referral growth.
  • The admin panel manages banners, tickets, and participating store locations, and issues auto-generated credentials for field staff.

Why it's interesting

It's a full promotional-marketing product, not just a scanner — entry tracking, drawing logic, notifications, and admin-side campaign management all working together.
