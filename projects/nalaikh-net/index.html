NalaikhNet Civic Platform

A district e-government platform: a public news & services portal, a mobile app, a citizen Q&A module, a registration module, and the admin panel that ties it together.

Overview

This is the largest project family in the portfolio — six separate repositories that together form one civic platform for a district, covering the public-facing site, a mobile companion app, and the internal tools staff use to run it.

What it does

  • Public portal — news and announcements, banners, a company/business directory, and a citizen complaint submission form, with COVID-era information pages from when it was first built.
  • Mobile app — an Expo/React Native app mirroring the portal's core features for residents, with offline-friendly local storage.
  • Citizen Q&A module — a separate signed-in flow where residents submit questions to the district office and track responses.
  • Registration module — a dedicated citizen registration flow, shipped as both a web app and its own mobile app.
  • Admin dashboard — staff sign in to manage users, posts, banners, and submitted complaints/questions, with its own authentication separate from citizen accounts.

Why it's interesting

It's a full multi-surface product — web, mobile, and admin — sharing one backend and one underlying data model, built to match how a district office actually operates rather than as a single monolithic app.

Tech Stack
