Overview

This is the largest project family in the portfolio — several separate repositories that together form one civic platform for a district, covering the public-facing site, a mobile companion app, and the internal tools staff use to run it.

What it does

  • Public portal — news and announcements, banners, a company/business directory, and a citizen complaint submission form, with COVID-era information pages from when it was first built.
  • Mobile app — an Expo/React Native app with a news feed, a classifieds/ads section (post and browse listings), a "free time" library of reading recommendations, and a resident profile, all behind a bottom tab bar.
  • Registration module — a dedicated citizen registration web flow for onboarding residents into the platform.
  • Admin dashboard — staff sign in to manage users, posts, banners, and submitted complaints/questions, with its own authentication separate from citizen accounts.

Why it's interesting

It's a full multi-surface product — web, mobile, and admin — sharing one backend and one underlying data model, built to match how a district office actually operates rather than as a single monolithic app.
