Geo Mongol

A full-stack platform for a geodesy and land-surveying engineering company — public marketing site, client portal, webshop, and internal tooling, all in one codebase.

Overview

Geo Mongol is a production platform I built and continue to maintain for a Mongolian geodesy and engineering services company, live at geo-mongol.mn. It's a monorepo with a React frontend and a Node/Express backend, covering the company's public presence, its client-facing services, and internal operations.

What it does

  • Public marketing pages describing the company's engineering services, with a full multi-language setup (i18next) for serving both Mongolian and international clients.
  • A client portal ("Client" module) where customers can track their surveying jobs.
  • A webshop ("Shop" module) for selling geodesy-related products and services.
  • An internal "Lab" module for the engineering team's own workflows.
  • Interactive maps (Mapbox / Google Maps) for visualizing survey sites and drone coverage areas.
  • Document generation: PDF reports and Word-to-web document rendering (mammoth) for delivering technical reports to clients.
  • Rich content editing for staff via TinyMCE, with image compression and HEIC conversion handled automatically on upload.

Why it's interesting

This is the most actively maintained project in the portfolio — commits are still landing regularly. It's also the clearest example of my architecture background feeding directly into the product: the client and lab modules are built around how a geodesy/surveying company actually works, not a generic CRUD template.
