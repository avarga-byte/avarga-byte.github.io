Web App

Platica

A Stack Overflow-style Q&A site for Mongolian speakers — ask a question in Mongolian, get answers from other users, in a community built around the language.

↑ A rebuilt copy of the frontend running live above, Matrix rain background and all — try the question box (posting requires the production API).

Overview

The idea behind Platica was straightforward: Stack Overflow and its peers are overwhelmingly English-first, and there wasn't a real Mongolian-language equivalent. Platica is a standalone attempt at one — a place to ask and answer questions in Mongolian, independent of any of the other client projects in this portfolio.

What it does

  • Post a question ("Асуулт тавих") and have other users answer it.
  • Sign up, sign in, and maintain a profile with a generated avatar (dicebear).
  • Browse and respond to open questions from the community.
  • A distinctive "Matrix rain" visual theme rather than a generic forum look.

Why it's interesting

It's the one project in this portfolio built purely as a product idea rather than for a specific client — betting that a Mongolian-language Q&A community was worth building on its own.
